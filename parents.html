Parent Area

Made for little explorers & their families 🌱

Little Sprout is designed to be a safe, offline-first learning environment for children ages 2–3.

Features for Parents

We've included a dedicated area in the app to help you manage your child's experience and keep track of their progress.

📊 Learning Progress

See which activities your child explores most. We track play counts and completions for every category, from Alphabet to Vehicles.

⚙️ Custom Settings

Adjust sound effects, background music, and autoplay preferences. Use Quiet Mode for a calmer learning session.

🛡️ Privacy & Safety

Little Sprout works entirely offline. No data is transmitted to our servers, and no third-party tracking or advertising is used.

📜 Content & Credits

View information about the artwork, audio, and technology that brings Little Sprout to life.

Parental Gate

To keep the learning experience focused on your child, all external links and parent settings are protected by a simple parental gate. This ensures that little explorers stay safely within the app while you maintain full control.

Offline & Ad-Free

We believe early learning should be free from distractions. Little Sprout contains no advertisements and requires no internet connection for any of its learning activities.
