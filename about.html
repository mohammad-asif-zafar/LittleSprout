About the app

Little Sprout

Early learning for little explorers.

App information

  • Recommended age: 2–3 years
  • Platforms: Android and iOS
  • Technology: Compose Multiplatform / Kotlin Multiplatform
  • Version: 1.0

What children can explore

Little Sprout includes learning activities for alphabet, numbers, colors, shapes, music, animals, birds, fruits, and vehicles. The parent area includes progress, settings, privacy and safety, content credits, contact, and app information screens.

Developer information

The project does not identify a developer or company name. The existing in-app support flow uses mohammadasifzafar000@gmail.com and links to https://mohammad-asif-zafar.github.io/LittleSprout/index.html.
